Cookie Policy

Understanding how we use cookies to improve your experience

Last Updated: November 29, 2025

Toronto Home Inspection Corp.

At Toronto Home Inspection Corp., we believe in being clear and open about how we collect and use data related to you. This Cookie Policy explains how and why we use cookies, web beacons, pixels, and other tracking technologies (collectively "Cookies") when you visit our website.

01

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer, smartphone, or other device when you visit a website. They allow the website to recognize your device, store your preferences, and improve your overall experience.

02

Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies for several reasons, ranging from making the website function properly (technical reasons) to helping us analyze how our site is performing in search engines (SEO and analytics).

We use the following categories of cookies:

A. Essential (Strictly Necessary) Cookies

These cookies are vital for the website to function. They are set by our Web Hosting Partners and infrastructure providers. Without them, basic functions like page navigation, security, and load balancing would not work. You cannot opt out of these cookies if you wish to use our site.

B. Analytics and SEO Cookies

We use Search Engine Optimization (SEO) tools to help us understand how people find our business in Toronto. These cookies collect information anonymously, such as:

  • Which pages you visit most often.
  • How long you spend on the site.
  • Whether you found us via Google, Bing, or a direct link.
  • If you encounter any error messages.

We use this data to fix technical issues and optimize our content to better serve homeowners and buyers.
