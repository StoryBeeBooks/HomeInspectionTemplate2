Privacy Policy

Your privacy and trust are important to us

Effective Date: November 29, 2025

At TO Inspect Ltd. ("we," "us," or "our"), we are committed to maintaining the accuracy, confidentiality, and security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy describes the personal information that we collect from or about you, how we use and to whom we disclose that information, and how you can correct or access it.

1 Information We Collect

We collect personal information that is necessary to provide our home inspection services and run our business. This includes:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, and current mailing address.
  • Property Information: Address of the property to be inspected, access details (lockbox codes), and specific concerns regarding the property.
  • Transaction Information: Services purchased, pricing, and payment details.
  • Real Estate Professional Information: Name and contact details of the Real Estate Agent representing you (if applicable).
Note: We do not store full credit card numbers on our servers; payments are processed through secure third-party payment gateways (e.g., Stripe, Square).

2 How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information for the following purposes:

  • To schedule and perform home inspection services.
  • To generate and deliver the Inspection Report.
  • To communicate with you regarding appointments, quotes, and follow-up questions.
  • To process payments and issue receipts.
  • To verify your identity and prevent fraud.
  • To comply with legal, insurance, and regulatory requirements (including tax reporting).

3 Disclosure of Information

We value your trust. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

We may disclose your personal information in the following limited circumstances:

  • To Your Realtor: We may share the Inspection Report or summary with your Real Estate Agent only with your verbal or written consent.
  • Service Providers: We may transfer data to trusted third-party service providers who assist us in running our business (e.g., report-writing software companies like Horizon/Spectora, payment processors, or cloud storage providers). These providers are contractually bound to protect your information.
  • Legal & Insurance: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to our insurance providers in the event of a liability claim.
  • The Seller: We do not share the inspection report with the seller or the seller's agent without your explicit permission.

4 Storage and Retention

Your personal information and Inspection Reports are stored electronically on secure servers.

  • Retention Period: We retain your personal information and inspection reports for a minimum period of seven (7) years to comply with Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) requirements and professional liability (insurance) standards.
  • After the retention period expires, data is securely destroyed or anonymized.

5 Security Safeguards

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data against loss, theft, and unauthorized access. This includes:

  • Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) encryption on our website.
  • Password-protected software and devices.
  • Restricted access to personal information (only authorized personnel have access).

6 Cookies and Website Data

When you visit our website, we may use "cookies" to analyze web traffic and improve your experience. These cookies do not collect personally identifiable information.

You can choose to decline cookies through your browser settings, though this may affect your ability to use certain features of our site.

7 Access and Correction

You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you and to request corrections if you believe it is inaccurate.

To make a request, please contact our Privacy Officer at the email listed below.

8 Changes to this Policy

We reserve the right to modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date."
