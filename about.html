Meet Michael Heart, RHI

Michael Heart is a Registered Home Inspector (RHI) and the founder of TO Inspect Ltd., serving homeowners, buyers, and real estate professionals throughout Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area. With a meticulous eye for detail and a passion for helping clients make informed decisions, Michael has built his reputation on thoroughness, transparency, and exceptional client education.

Before entering the home inspection industry, Michael spent years in the construction and renovation sector, gaining hands-on experience with residential building systems from foundations to rooftops. This practical background gives him unique insights into how homes are built, where problems commonly develop, and what maintenance practices extend the life of a property.

As a member of the Ontario Association of Home Inspectors (OAHI) and holder of multiple professional certifications—including WETT certification for wood-burning appliances and Level 1 Thermography for infrared imaging—Michael stays at the forefront of industry standards and inspection technology. He regularly attends continuing education seminars and keeps current with changes to the Ontario Building Code.

Michael is also recognized as a Tarion warranty expert, having conducted hundreds of Pre-Delivery Inspections (PDIs) and Year-End warranty inspections for new home buyers. His familiarity with Tarion's construction performance guidelines helps clients identify warrantable deficiencies and navigate the warranty claim process effectively.

When he's not inspecting properties, Michael enjoys sharing his knowledge through the Knowledge Share section of this website, where he publishes articles on seasonal maintenance tips, common inspection findings, and homeowner education. His philosophy is simple: an informed homeowner is an empowered homeowner.

Our Philosophy

At TO Inspect Ltd., we understand that a home is likely the largest purchase you will ever make. Our approach is built on four core principles.

Unbiased & Independent

We are strictly inspectors—never contractors. We have no financial interest in recommending repairs or services. Our only job is to provide you with an honest, unbiased assessment of the property's condition so you can make your decision with confidence.

Standards & Technology

We inspect according to OAHI and CAN/CSA-A770-16 standards. We also utilize thermal imaging technology to detect issues invisible to the naked eye—moisture intrusion, insulation gaps, electrical hot spots, and more—giving you a more complete picture of the property.

Local Expertise

We know Toronto and the GTA. From century homes in the Annex to modern condos in Liberty Village, from suburban builds in Markham to waterfront properties in Oakville—we understand the unique challenges and characteristics of properties in our region.
